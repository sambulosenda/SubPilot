# Product

## Register

brand

(Scope: this file governs the web/marketing surfaces in `landing/`. The iOS app itself follows Apple HIG / Liquid Glass and is out of impeccable's scope.)

## Users

iPhone users arriving from Product Hunt, App Store search, or social, already irritated by subscription creep: forgotten free trials, surprise renewals, no idea what the monthly total is. They are privacy-conscious enough to hesitate before connecting a bank account to a tracker. They decide in seconds; the page's one job is an App Store tap.

## Product Purpose

SubPilot is a subscription tracker for iPhone that never sees your data. Receipts and statements are parsed on-device with Apple Intelligence; sync is private iCloud; there is no account because there is no server. Free with a Pro tier (StoreKit 2). Success for the landing page = App Store tap-through; secondary: feature-board visits and press/editorial credibility.

## Brand Personality

Bold, direct, trustworthy. Speaks plainly about money and privacy; confident enough to use huge type and silence instead of decoration. Never corporate, never pleading, never gimmicky.

## Anti-references

- Fintech-navy corporate (navy + gold gradients, stock photography, "trusted by" walls)
- Aggregator marketing à la Rocket Money / Truebill: bank-login screenshots, savings-claim hero numbers
- Generic SaaS template: purple gradients, identical icon-card grids, gradient text
- Crypto-neon dark mode

## Design Principles

1. Privacy is the plot, not a footnote. "No account, no ads, no tracking" gets hero-grade treatment.
2. Show the actual app. Real screenshots over illustrations; the product is the proof.
3. Conversion beats spectacle. Nothing may delay or obscure the App Store badge; effects must never cost a tap.
4. One voice, few words. Plain claims a person can verify ("0 bytes collected"), no marketing filler.
5. Apple-native credibility. The page should feel like it was made by someone who sweats iOS details, because the app was.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. `prefers-reduced-motion` fully honoured (animations and parallax off, content visible). Keyboard reachable CTAs, descriptive alt text on app screenshots. No motion that blocks reading; no autoplaying media.
