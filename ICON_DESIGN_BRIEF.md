# SubHelm — App Icon Design Brief

## App
**SubHelm: Subscription Tracker** (iOS, Finance category). Keeps every
subscription in one place — renewal reminders, real monthly total, receipt
scan — so a forgotten free trial never becomes a surprise charge.

## Audience
Consumers (20s–40s) with 5–20 subscriptions who want control over recurring
spend. They compare us against Bobby, Rocket Money, and generic "subscription
manager" apps in search results.

## Brand
- Primary teal: **#0D9488** (deep teal companion #074E48; light accent #1FC3AE)
- App UI: iOS 26 Liquid Glass, dark-leaning, premium/minimal
- Store screenshots use a teal gradient background — icon must sit
  comfortably next to them on the product page
- Mood: **trustworthy, calm-in-control, premium** — not loud fintech, not
  playful cartoon

## What the icon should convey
One idea: **"recurring payments, under control."** Think a single bold mark
hinting at cycles/renewal (loop, orbit, radar sweep, circular arrow) or
calm oversight — NOT a literal dollar sign, wallet, or bell.

## Current icon (to improve on, not necessarily keep)
Dark navy rounded square, white interlocked "SP" monogram, small teal arrow.
Problems: monogram reads as text-like clutter at 60px; "SP" means nothing to
a new user; arrow suggests growth/stocks, wrong association.

## Hard requirements
- Single memorable mark, **max 2 elements**, describable in 3 words
- **No text/letters preferred** (if a letterform is used, it must read as a
  shape first)
- Legible at **60×60px** — test every concept at that size in a mock App
  Store search row next to Bobby and Rocket Money
- High contrast on BOTH white (light mode) and dark (dark mode) App Store
  backgrounds — avoid pure-white and near-black flat fills
- Must work as favicon, push-notification mark, social avatar

## Differentiate from (look these up)
- **Bobby** — dark blue, white "b" letterform
- **Rocket Money** — purple/pink gradient, rocket
- **Subby / Subscriptions: Tracker-type apps** — blue lists/checkmarks
- Finance category default = blue & conservative → our teal is the wedge;
  own it boldly rather than adding more blue

## Avoid
- Dollar/currency signs, piggy banks, bells, generic charts
- Multi-element scenes, thin line-art that vanishes at small size
- Gradients so busy the silhouette is lost
- Anything resembling Rocket Money's gradient or Bobby's letter-on-dark

## Deliverables
1. **3 distinct concepts** at 1024×1024px (e.g. one mark-on-teal, one
   teal-mark-on-dark, one wildcard)
2. Each concept shown at 60×60px in an App Store search mockup (light + dark)
3. Final master: **1024×1024 PNG, no alpha, square corners** (Apple masks it)
4. Source file (Figma/vector) + mark isolated on transparent for reuse

## Process note
We'll A/B test 2–3 finalists via App Store Product Page Optimization, so
variants that differ in ONE variable (e.g. same mark, dark vs teal
background) are extra valuable.
