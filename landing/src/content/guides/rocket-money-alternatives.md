People search for Rocket Money alternatives for two different reasons, and they lead to different answers. Some want the same bank-connected experience from a different brand. Others like the idea of a subscription tracker but not the part where it reads their bank account. This guide is mostly for the second group, because that's the choice we understand best; our own app is in that camp, and we say so where relevant.

## What you're actually replacing

Rocket Money's core loop: connect your bank, let it detect recurring charges, get alerts, optionally pay for extras like bill negotiation. It does this well. The reasons people look elsewhere tend to be:

- **The data trade.** Detection means sharing your transaction history, and your full spending pattern is exactly the kind of data that gets monetised in aggregate across this industry.
- **Coverage gaps.** It sees the accounts you connect, nothing else: the card you didn't link, PayPal, a family member's account, app-store bundles.
- **Paying a subscription to manage subscriptions.** The free tier is real, but the useful extras sit behind Premium, and that irony wears on people.

If none of these bother you, keep Rocket Money; switching brands within the same bank-connected model rarely improves much. If they do, the alternatives below all work without a single bank credential.

## Apple's built-in Subscriptions list, free

**Settings, your name, Subscriptions.** Every Apple-billed subscription with renewal dates and a cancel button. No app to install. Its blind spot is everything billed outside Apple, which for most people is the bigger half. Start here anyway; it takes one minute and shrinks the problem.

## Bobby, the one-time-purchase minimalist

Manual entry against a large service library, data on your device, a small one-time unlock instead of a subscription. The best pick if you want the least app and the simplest possible price. You do all entry yourself, and reminders are basic by design.

## SubTracker, free manual tracking at volume

Unlimited manual tracking on the free tier, with reminders in the paid Plus plan. A fair choice for a long list on a budget.

## SubHelm, the privacy-first pick (ours)

[SubHelm](/) is our app, built specifically as the anti-bank-login tracker, so weigh this entry accordingly. No account, no server, nothing to log in to: your list stays on the iPhone and syncs through your own iCloud. Entry is the part we worked hardest on, because manual trackers live or die there: a 200+ service catalogue, quick manual add, or receipt scanning read entirely on-device. It keeps one honest monthly and yearly total across currencies and reminds you before every renewal and before [trials convert](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/). Free, with an optional Pro upgrade.

The honest limitation is the same as every app in this list: nothing is auto-detected, so setup costs a few minutes. Why we think that trade is right, in detail: [do you need to connect your bank to track subscriptions?](/guides/subscription-tracker-without-bank-account/)

## Choosing in one paragraph

Stay with Rocket Money if automatic detection matters more than where your transaction data goes. Use Apple's list if your subscriptions are mostly Apple-billed. Pick Bobby for a one-time price and maximum simplicity, SubTracker for free volume, and SubHelm if you want entry that doesn't feel like a chore, reminders that catch trials, and a tracker that couldn't read your bank account even if it wanted to. Whichever you choose, do the [yearly-cost audit](/guides/what-subscriptions-really-cost/) once; that's where the actual savings are.
