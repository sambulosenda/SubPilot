The moment you start a free trial is the moment to decide how it ends. Set a reminder **two days before the trial expires** the same minute you sign up, and the silent-conversion problem disappears. Everything else in this guide is detail.

## Why trials convert silently

A free trial is priced at zero because the company expects a meaningful share of people to forget it. The email warning you before conversion is sometimes sent, sometimes not, and often lands in a folder you don't check. Nothing on your phone announces the change; the first sign is usually the charge itself, and by then you've bought a month you didn't want.

None of this is an accident, which is why the fix has to be on your side.

## The two-day rule

Set the reminder for **two days before** the end date, not the end date itself:

- Apple recommends cancelling App Store trials at least 24 hours before renewal; cutting it to the final day risks paying anyway.
- A two-day margin covers time zones, "I'll do it tonight", and the reminder firing while you're busy.
- Cancelling a trial early almost never ends it early. On the App Store and with most direct billers, the trial runs to its end date after you cancel, so acting on the reminder costs you nothing.

## Where to see a trial's real end date

For anything billed through Apple: **Settings, your name, Subscriptions**, tap the subscription, and the exact conversion date and paid price are printed there. That date is authoritative; use it, not your memory of "about a month".

For direct-billed trials, the end date is in the signup confirmation email. If you can't find it, the account's billing page on the company website will show the next charge date.

## Making it automatic

The manual version works: phone calendar, two days early, alert on. Its weakness is that it depends on you doing it every single time, including the trial you start half-asleep because it was the only way past a paywall.

The automatic version is an app whose job this is. In [SubHelm](/) you mark a subscription as a trial when you add it, and it reminds you before the trial converts and before every renewal after that. The trial's cost also shows up in [your real monthly total](/guides/what-subscriptions-really-cost/) from day one, at the price it will convert to, so a "free" trial never reads as free.

## If a trial already converted

- Cancel it now so it stops at the end of the paid period; the [cancellation guide](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/) covers both Apple and direct billing.
- If it converted in the last few days and you never used the paid period, ask for a refund. Apple handles App Store refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com, and plenty of direct billers will refund an obviously unused first charge if you ask politely.
- Then set the reminder habit, because the next trial is never far away.
