Paramount+ is a textbook example of the modern cancellation problem: the same service can be billed by Paramount, by Apple, or by Amazon as a Prime Video Channel, and each biller has its own cancel button. People fail to cancel not because it's hard but because they're pressing the button in the wrong shop.

## Find your biller in ten seconds

Check the charge on your card or your email receipts. Paramount's own name means direct. Apple means you subscribed in-app on an iPhone, iPad or Apple TV. Amazon means it's a Prime Video Channel.

## Direct subscription

1. Sign in on the Paramount+ website.
2. Open your account settings and find the subscription section.
3. Choose cancel and confirm; streaming runs to the end of the paid period.

## Through Apple

**Settings, your name, Subscriptions**, tap Paramount+, cancel. Details and caveats in the [iPhone cancellation guide](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/).

## As an Amazon Prime Video Channel

Channel subscriptions live in your **Amazon account**, under memberships and subscriptions, not on Paramount's site. Cancel the channel there. This is the one people miss for months, because the charge hides inside Amazon's billing; the [Amazon Prime guide](/cancel/amazon-prime/) covers the channels trap in general.

## Worth knowing

Subscribing to the same streamer through two billers by accident is common; if two different companies charge you amounts that look like Paramount+, one is a duplicate. A [single tracked list of every subscription](/), whoever bills it, is how duplicates get caught before they cost a year of double payments.
