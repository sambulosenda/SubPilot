Audible has one rule that outranks the cancel steps: **spend your credits first.** Unused credits are typically forfeited when a membership ends, and cancelling with three credits banked is like leaving three audiobooks on the table.

## Before you cancel

1. Check your credit balance in your Audible account.
2. Spend every credit; the books they buy are yours to keep after cancellation, in your library forever.
3. Then cancel.

## Cancelling Audible

1. Sign in at **audible.com** (or your regional Audible site) in a browser; the membership controls are on the website, not in the iOS app.
2. Open your **account details**, then the membership section.
3. Choose **Cancel membership** and click through the retention offers to confirm.

Membership benefits run to the end of the paid period. Your purchased library survives; the monthly credit stops.

## Consider pausing instead

If the problem is a backlog rather than the service, Audible offers a **plan pause** for a few months, from the same membership area or via support. A pause keeps your credits and member pricing without new charges; just remember it resumes on its own, the same way [free trials convert](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/), so note the resume date somewhere that will actually remind you.

## Worth knowing

- If you subscribed to Audible through Apple, it cancels in **Settings, your name, Subscriptions** like any [Apple-billed subscription](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/).
- Audible retention offers are famously generous: a few months at a steep discount is commonly on the exit path. Take it only if you'd genuinely use it; a discounted subscription you don't use is still [money per year](/guides/what-subscriptions-really-cost/), just less of it.
