Disney+ is one of the services where the cancel button genuinely moves around: you may be billed by Disney directly, by Apple, or as part of a bundle. Finding out who bills you is the whole job; the cancelling itself takes a minute.

## Step one: check who bills you

Look at where the charge lands. A Disney+ line on your card means direct billing. An Apple charge means you subscribed through the App Store. A Hulu, Max or telecoms charge means a bundle, and the bundle owner is who you cancel with.

## If Disney bills you directly

1. Sign in at the Disney+ website in a browser.
2. Open your account settings and select your subscription.
3. Choose cancel and confirm.

You keep watching until the end of the paid period.

## If you subscribed through Apple

Open **Settings, your name, Subscriptions**, tap Disney+, and cancel there. The [general iPhone guide](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/) covers the details, including why deleting the app changes nothing.

## Worth knowing

- **Bundles cancel as a unit.** If your Disney+ comes in a duo or trio bundle with Hulu or ESPN+, cancelling the bundle ends all of it; check what you'd actually lose first.
- **Annual plans refund poorly.** Cancelling an annual plan mid-year generally just stops renewal rather than refunding the remainder, so set [a reminder before the yearly date](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/) instead of paying for a thirteenth month you didn't want.
