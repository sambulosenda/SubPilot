HBO Max subscriptions arrive from more directions than most: directly from the service, through Apple or Google, or bundled by a TV or mobile provider. The charge on your statement is the map: the service's own name means direct, Apple means App Store, and a provider's name means the provider owns the cancellation.

## If you're billed directly

1. Sign in on the HBO Max website in a browser.
2. Open your account or subscription settings.
3. Choose to cancel and confirm; access continues until the paid period ends.

## If you subscribed through Apple

**Settings, your name, Subscriptions**, tap the subscription, cancel. The [general iPhone guide](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/) applies, including the rule that deleting the app changes nothing about billing.

## If it came from your TV or mobile provider

Bundled subscriptions cancel with whoever bills you, through the provider's account portal or support line. The streaming app itself often can't end these at all, which is confusing by design rather than by accident.

## Worth knowing

- **Ad-supported downgrades** save most of the money if price is the issue; look for the plan-change option before the cancel button.
- Streaming services in this tier are strong candidates for rotation: subscribe for the show, cancel at the finale, return next season. Nothing is lost in between, and [a renewal reminder](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/) turns rotation from an intention into a system. If you rotate several streamers, [one list with all their dates](/) is the difference between a plan and a mess.
