Hulu cancellation depends on who bills you, and with Hulu there are three realistic answers: Hulu itself, Apple, or Disney, because Hulu now travels inside Disney's bundles as often as it travels alone.

## If Hulu bills you directly

1. Sign in at **hulu.com** in a browser and open your **Account** page.
2. Under your subscription, choose **Cancel**.
3. Push through the retention offers and confirm.

You keep watching until the current period ends. Hulu also offers a pause option on the way out; a pause is fine if it's deliberate, but it resumes billing automatically, so treat it like the [trial-conversion problem](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/) and set a reminder.

## If you subscribed through Apple

Hulu appears in **Settings, your name, Subscriptions**; cancel it there. The [iPhone guide](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/) covers the quirks.

## If Hulu came in a Disney bundle

When Hulu is part of a Disney+ bundle, the subscription is managed from the **Disney+ account**, not Hulu's site; your Hulu account page will say as much. Cancelling the bundle ends Hulu, Disney+ and any other bundled service at once, so decide what you're keeping first; the [Disney+ guide](/cancel/disney-plus/) has the details.

## Worth knowing

Hulu's cheaper with-ads tier exists as a downgrade path: if the cancellation reason is price rather than the content, switching tiers on the Account page saves most of the money without losing the service.
