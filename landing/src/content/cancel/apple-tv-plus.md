Apple TV+ is the easy case: it's always billed by Apple, so the cancel button is always in the same place, on every device signed into your Apple account.

## Cancelling Apple TV+

1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone and tap your name.
2. Tap **Subscriptions**, then **Apple TV+**.
3. Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

You keep streaming until the end of the current billing period. The same screen works for every other Apple-billed subscription, which is why it's worth [knowing your way around it](/guides/cancel-subscriptions-iphone/) in general.

## If you don't see Apple TV+ in the list

You're probably paying for it through **Apple One**, the bundle that wraps TV+, Music, iCloud+ and more into one charge. In that case TV+ has no separate line to cancel:

- To drop the whole bundle, cancel **Apple One** in the same Subscriptions screen.
- To keep some services, cancel Apple One and re-subscribe individually to the ones you want, or switch bundle tiers.

Before dropping Apple One entirely, check your iCloud storage: if the bundle was supplying your storage plan, cancelling can leave you over quota, and Apple will start nagging immediately.

## Worth knowing

Apple TV+ is a strong candidate for the subscribe-binge-cancel cycle: cancel when the show you came for ends, re-subscribe when the next season lands. Nothing about your account is lost in between, and [a renewal reminder](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/) makes the cycle deliberate instead of accidental.
