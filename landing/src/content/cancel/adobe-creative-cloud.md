Adobe Creative Cloud is the cancellation with a catch: the popular plan is an **annual contract paid monthly**, and ending it mid-year usually triggers an early-termination fee. Knowing the fee rules before you press cancel is worth real money here.

## The fee rules, first

- **Within 14 days of starting or renewing:** cancel and you're entitled to a full refund of what you've paid for the new term. This is the free exit; if you're near a renewal that just happened, act fast.
- **Mid-contract on an annual-paid-monthly plan:** Adobe charges a portion of the remaining months as an early-termination fee. The cancellation flow shows the exact figure before you confirm; check it, because sometimes waiting a month or two changes the maths.
- **True month-to-month plans:** no fee, they just end at the period close. Month-to-month costs more per month, which is exactly the flexibility you're paying for.

## Cancelling

1. Sign in at **adobe.com** and open your **account page**.
2. Under Plans, choose **Manage plan**, then **Cancel your plan**.
3. Read the fee-or-refund screen carefully, then confirm.

If the fee is steep, two legitimate alternatives: downgrade to a cheaper plan, like Photography, from the same Manage plan screen, or ride out the remaining months and cancel in the renewal window. Set [a reminder before the renewal date](/guides/never-pay-for-a-forgotten-free-trial/) so the window doesn't slip past; an annual Adobe renewal is one of the most expensive charges to forget, and it belongs in [your yearly total](/guides/what-subscriptions-really-cost/) at its full, honest number.

## Worth knowing

Adobe's flow will offer discounts and free months to keep you; if a discount genuinely solves your problem, take it, but note the date the discount ends, because the price snaps back without ceremony.
